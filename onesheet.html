A Guide to Democracy In Music

OPEN SOURCE · FREE DEMOCRACYINMUSIC.COM

What follows is a set of working prototypes for communal composition. Each explores a different democratic mechanism applied to the creation of music: averaging, continuous voting, spatial voting, layered choice, redistricting, embodied movement. They run in a browser. You can play them. None are finished pieces of music. They are instruments you can play.

Six Prototypes
DP·01
Piano
Democratic Instrument
A digital piano played by many at once. Each person picks a note; the keyboard plays back the mean, the mode, or a chord of every choice. Voters can be one or many; voices can be one or several.
Play →
MM·01
Mean Mode
Continuous Pitch
The same idea, freed from twelve named keys. A river of votes on a continuous pitch line, heard as a single mean, as a cluster of modes, or as a chord made of the loudest voices.
Play →
MI·01
Miro
Spatial Voting
A cerulean canvas. Each touch is a vote; overlapping votes form peaks in a 3D landscape. Raise the water threshold and only the strongest peaks survive to be sonified. Inspired by Miró’s Bleu II.
Play →
DA·01
Assembly
Layered Composition
Five layers (rhythm, bass, chords, melody, texture), four variations each. Preview a block, cast your vote, hear what the crowd chose. The composition builds one collective decision at a time.
Play →
PA·01
Parliament
Redistricting
Five seats. The voters never move. The districts do. Drag the seeds and watch the parliament — and the harmony it produces — re-shape in real time. Compare fair, packed, and stacked maps.
Play →
CH·01
Choreography
Body as Instrument
Dancers, each with their own beat, drift together or apart. Drag SYNC up and they entrain; drag it down and they scatter. Their bodies are the instrument; the sync fader is the score.
Play →

Core Idea

Music has always been communal but its creation and ownership rarely are. These prototypes explore what happens when democratic governance is applied directly to the act of making music. The process of creation becomes as democratic as the outcome.
