Six Prototypes
DP·01
Piano
Democratic Instrument
A digital piano played by many at once. Each person picks a note; the keyboard plays back the mean, the mode, or a chord of every choice.
MM·01
Mean Mode
Continuous Pitch
The same idea, freed from the keys. A river of votes on a continuous pitch line, heard as a single mean, a cluster of modes, or a chord.
MI·01
Miro
Spatial Voting
A cerulean canvas. Each touch is a vote, accumulating into peaks. A water threshold filters out the weak; the strongest peaks are sonified.
DA·01
Assembly
Layered Composition
Five layers, four variations each. Preview a block, cast a vote, hear what the crowd chose. The piece builds one decision at a time.
PA·01
Parliament
Redistricting
Five seats. The voters never move. The districts do. Drag the seeds and watch the parliament — and its harmony — re-shape around them.
CH·01
Choreography
Body as Instrument
Dancers, each with their own beat, drift together or apart. Their bodies are the instrument; sync is the score.
